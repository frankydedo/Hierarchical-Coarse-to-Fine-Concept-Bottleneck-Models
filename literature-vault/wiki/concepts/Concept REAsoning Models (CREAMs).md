# Concept REAsoning Models (CREAMs)

Concept REAsoning Models (CREAMs) are a variant of Concept Bottleneck Models that explicitly encode prior knowledge about how concepts relate to one another (C-C) and to the task (C->Y). By employing Structured Neural Networks, CREAMs enforce a reasoning graph (like a DAG) that restricts information flow, ensuring that predictions strictly follow expert-defined logical structures (such as mutual exclusivity or hierarchical dependencies).
