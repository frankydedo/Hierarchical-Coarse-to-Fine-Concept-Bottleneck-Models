# Unsupervised Concept Bases

Unsupervised Concept Bases represent clusters or latent representations learned automatically by a neural network without explicit human-provided concept labels. These bases might capture distinct visual traits or textures. Explainability methods attempt to map these unsupervised clusters back to human-understandable meaning post-hoc.
