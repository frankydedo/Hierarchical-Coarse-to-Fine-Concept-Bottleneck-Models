# Region-to-Prototype Contrastive Loss (RPC-Loss)

Region-to-Prototype Contrastive Loss (RPC-Loss) is a training objective that enhances the structural consistency between local image regions and semantic prototypes. It uses generated pseudo-labels to perform region-wise contrastive learning, ensuring prototypes focus heavily on target objects rather than background noise.
