# Bottleneck Concept Learner (BotCL)

Bottleneck Concept Learner (BotCL) is an unsupervised concept discovery framework that learns to represent an image purely by the presence or absence of concepts. It optimizes concepts for a target image classification task without requiring explicit concept supervision, relying instead on [[Self-Supervision over Concepts]] and regularization to ensure human-understandability.
