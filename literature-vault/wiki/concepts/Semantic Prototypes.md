# Semantic Prototypes

Semantic Prototypes are auxiliary representations that describe the human-understandable properties of a class. They are typically obtained independently of the visual data, for instance via manually annotated attribute vectors (e.g., "has tail", "is striped") or unsupervised word embeddings (e.g., Word2Vec) derived from class names. They act as the bridge to transfer knowledge to unseen classes in Zero-Shot Learning.
