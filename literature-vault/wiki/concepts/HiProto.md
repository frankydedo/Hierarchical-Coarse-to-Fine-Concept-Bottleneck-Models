# HiProto

HiProto is a prototype-driven, end-to-end interpretable architecture for object detection. It integrates [[Hierarchical Prototype Learning (HPL)|Hierarchical Prototype Learning]] into multi-scale feature extractors (like Feature Pyramid Networks) to provide semantic, class-guided representations at different feature scales, improving robustness under low-quality imaging conditions without relying on image enhancement pipelines.
