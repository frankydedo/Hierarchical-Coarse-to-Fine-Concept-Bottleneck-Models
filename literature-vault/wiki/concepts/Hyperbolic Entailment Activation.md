# Hyperbolic Entailment Activation

Hyperbolic Entailment Activation reformulates concept activation as an asymmetric geometric containment problem. A concept is activated if the image's embedding falls within the entailment cone of the concept in hyperbolic space. This naturally produces sparse, hierarchy-aware activations.
