# Property Bottleneck

A Property Bottleneck is a conceptual layer that predicts abstract, human-understandable characteristics (e.g., symmetry, visibility, or color) derived from the input or a preceding [[Segmentation Bottleneck]]. These properties are then used to inform the final task decision.

**Related Papers:**
- [[Lin_2022]]: Uses a property bottleneck based on ISUOG clinical criteria to evaluate the quality of ultrasound scans.
