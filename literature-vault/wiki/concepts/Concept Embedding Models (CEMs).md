# Concept Embedding Models (CEMs)

Concept Embedding Models (CEMs) map inputs to high-dimensional concept embeddings rather than scalar probabilities, providing concept-based explanations while achieving higher task accuracies than standard Concept Bottleneck Models (CBMs). They learn separate embeddings for the active and inactive states of a concept.
