# HIL-CBM

HIL-CBM (Hierarchical Interpretable Label-Free Concept Bottleneck Model) is an extension of the CBM architecture into a hierarchical framework. It enables both classification and explanation across multiple semantic levels without requiring relational concept annotations. The model aligns the abstraction level of its concept-based explanations with that of its predictions (e.g., from abstract to concrete).
