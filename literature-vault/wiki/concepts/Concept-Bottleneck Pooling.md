# Concept-Bottleneck Pooling

Concept-Bottleneck Pooling is a selective mechanism that aggregates latent features into semantic concepts, effectively filtering out irrelevant signals and spurious activations before they reach the classification stage. It acts similarly to max-pooling but over conceptual dimensions rather than spatial ones.
