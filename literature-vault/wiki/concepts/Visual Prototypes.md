# Visual Prototypes

Visual Prototypes are learned, representative feature vectors in the visual embedding space that capture the core appearance or morphological characteristics of an object class. In zero-shot learning, they are often learned to match the image features closely while maintaining discriminability from other classes.

**Related Papers:**
- [[Zhang_2019]]: Learns visual prototypes to represent seen and unseen classes and maps them to semantic prototypes.
