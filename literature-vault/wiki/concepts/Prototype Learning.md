# Prototype Learning

Prototype Learning is a machine learning technique where a model learns to represent data by identifying "prototypes"—representative examples or embeddings that capture the essential characteristics of a specific group or class.

In healthcare predictions, learning prototypes at various levels (e.g., patient, visit, or medical code) helps capture the intrinsic similarities among entities. For example, a patient-level prototype might capture a cohort of individuals with similar lifestyle habits or chronic conditions. Models can use these prototypes to offer interpretability by demonstrating which prototype a new sample is most similar to.
