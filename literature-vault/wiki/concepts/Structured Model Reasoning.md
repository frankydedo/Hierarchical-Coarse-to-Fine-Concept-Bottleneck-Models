# Structured Model Reasoning

Structured Model Reasoning refers to the practice of explicitly defining the dependencies between intermediate concepts in an interpretable model. Instead of treating concepts as conditionally independent, models with structured reasoning enforce specific interaction graphs (e.g., correlations, hierarchies, or mutually exclusive groups) to align the model's internal logic with human domain knowledge.
