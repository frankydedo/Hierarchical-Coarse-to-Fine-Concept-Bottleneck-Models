# Super-prototypes

Super-prototypes are higher-level, generalized representations learned from either visual or semantic prototypes. Because there can be overlap or shared structure between seen and unseen classes, super-prototypes bridge the visual and semantic domains and facilitate knowledge transfer. Aligning the structural consistency of super-prototypes in both spaces improves recognition performance.
