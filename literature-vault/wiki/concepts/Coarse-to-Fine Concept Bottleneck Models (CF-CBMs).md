# Coarse-to-Fine Concept Bottleneck Models (CF-CBMs)

Coarse-to-Fine Concept Bottleneck Models (CF-CBMs) are a hierarchical CBM architecture that discovers and utilizes concepts at multiple levels of granularity. They typically combine a high-level module (analyzing the whole image) with a low-level module (analyzing specific patches or regions) to capture both global semantic context and localized, fine-grained details.

**Related Papers:**
- [[Panousis_2023]]: Proposes CF-CBMs to solve the problem of concept over-abundance by hierarchically masking irrelevant patch attributes.
