# Explainable fetal ultrasound quality assessment with progressive concept bottleneck models

## Summary
The paper proposes [[Progressive Concept Bottleneck Models (P-CBMs)]] for assessing the quality of fetal ultrasound scans. To emulate the expert decision-making process ("seeing, conceiving, concluding"), the model uses a hierarchical sequence of bottlenecks. It first extracts visual [[Segmentation Bottleneck]]s (organs) and then uses these to predict [[Property Bottleneck]]s (ISUOG quality criteria), which are finally used for task prediction. This progressive structure reduces [[Concept Leakage]].

## Key Contributions
- **[[Progressive Concept Bottleneck Models (P-CBMs)]]:** A multi-stage CBM architecture tailored for complex visual tasks.
- Avoids unintended harm from [[Concept Leakage]] by strictly separating the visual segmentation from the higher-level property concepts.
- Provides holistic explanations aligned with clinical ISUOG standards.
- Achieves high generalization on external datasets and allows test-time interventions on both segmentation and property concepts.
