# Boosting Concept Bottleneck Models with Supervised, Hierarchical Concept Learning

## Summary
This paper proposes SUPCBM to mitigate [[Concept Leakage]] in Concept Bottleneck Models. By employing [[Label-Aware Concept Annotation]] and [[Concept Pooling]], along with a fixed intervention matrix, the model explicitly pairs relevant concepts with labels, preventing the label predictor from exploiting unintended information.

## Key Contributions
- Introduces [[SUPCBM]], a paradigm that supervises concept prediction with class labels.
- Constructs a two-level concept hierarchy (perceptual and descriptive) using LLMs.
- Demonstrates superiority in interpretability and reduction of information leakage over SOTA CBMs.
