# Eliminating Information Leakage in Hard Concept Bottleneck Models with Supervised, Hierarchical Concept Learning

## Summary
The paper proposes SUPCBM, a novel paradigm for Concept Bottleneck Models that mitigates [[Concept Leakage]] (Information Leakage). It achieves this by introducing label supervision during concept prediction and using a fixed intervention matrix instead of a learned label predictor. Furthermore, it advocates for a two-level hierarchical concept set consisting of nounal perceptual concepts and adjectival descriptive concepts, automatically constructed using Large Language Models like GPT-4.

## Key Contributions
- **SUPCBM:** Fuses concept prediction and label prediction to eliminate information leakage, achieving label prediction via an intervention matrix.
- **[[Label-Aware Concept Annotation]]:** Only annotates training images with concepts that are actively involved in predicting the ground-truth label.
- **[[Concept Pooling]]:** A selective strategy analogous to max pooling that trims irrelevant concepts, focusing only on the most important descriptive concepts to reduce hard information leakage.
- Achieves high interpretability and interoperability while outperforming state-of-the-art CBMs across diverse datasets.
