# Learning Bottleneck Concepts in Image Classification

## Summary
The paper proposes the [[Bottleneck Concept Learner (BotCL)]] to automatically discover visual concepts for image classification without explicit concept-level supervision. BotCL represents an image purely by the presence or absence of concepts using [[Slot Attention]]. It relies on [[Self-Supervision over Concepts]] (using contrastive and reconstruction losses) and specific regularizers (individual consistency and mutual distinctiveness) to ensure the discovered concepts are human-understandable and distinguishable.

## Key Contributions
- **[[Bottleneck Concept Learner (BotCL)]]:** A framework for unsupervised concept discovery within a bottleneck layer.
- Employs [[Slot Attention]] to localize concept representations.
- Proposes [[Self-Supervision over Concepts]] using contrastive learning, eliminating the need for expensive concept annotations.
- Demonstrates competitive classification accuracy and interpretable discovered concepts on datasets like CUB200 and ImageNet.
