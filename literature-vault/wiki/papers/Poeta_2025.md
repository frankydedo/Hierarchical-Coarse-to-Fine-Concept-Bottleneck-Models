# Concept-based Explainable Artificial Intelligence: A Survey

## Summary
This survey provides a comprehensive overview of [[Concept-based Explainable Artificial Intelligence (C-XAI)]]. It categorizes the field based on whether methods are post-hoc or explainable-by-design, and based on the type of concepts used, including [[Symbolic Concepts]], [[Unsupervised Concept Bases]], [[Hierarchical Prototypes|Prototypes]], and [[Textual Concepts]]. The survey outlines nine categories of C-XAI and offers guidelines for selecting appropriate methods based on model flexibility and data availability.

## Key Contributions
- Systematic taxonomy of [[Concept-based Explainable Artificial Intelligence (C-XAI)]] methods.
- Clearly defines different types of concepts: [[Symbolic Concepts]], [[Unsupervised Concept Bases]], Prototypes, and [[Textual Concepts]].
- Compares post-hoc explanation methods (which explain an existing model) with explainable-by-design models (which incorporate concepts directly into their architecture).
- Reviews quantitative evaluation metrics and available datasets for C-XAI.
