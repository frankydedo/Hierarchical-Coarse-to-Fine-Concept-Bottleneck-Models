# PRISMA Protocol

## Research Questions
1. What are the current concept-based explainable models, specifically focusing on concept bottleneck models (CBMs)?
2. How is coarse-to-fine concept discovery currently approached in the literature?
3. What are the existing hierarchical prototype-based methods for explainability?
4. How do current methods evaluate classification errors and semantic mistake severity in a hierarchy-aware manner?

## Inclusion Criteria
- Studies proposing or evaluating hierarchical concept-based models (e.g., hierarchical CBMs, hierarchical prototypical networks).
- Studies focusing on coarse-to-fine concept discovery and abstraction.
- Studies proposing or utilizing hierarchy-aware evaluation metrics for semantic mistakes.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Studies on flat concept-based models with no hierarchical component, unless used as a foundational baseline.
- Hierarchical models that lack an explainable/interpretable concept bottleneck or prototype design.

## Search Strings
- `("concept bottleneck model" OR "CBM") AND "hierarchical"`
- `"coarse-to-fine" AND "concept discovery"`
- `"hierarchical prototype" OR "hierarchical prototypical network"`
- `"hierarchy-aware evaluation" OR "semantic mistake severity"`
