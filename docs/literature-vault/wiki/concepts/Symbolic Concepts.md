# Symbolic Concepts

Symbolic Concepts are pre-defined, human-understandable attributes or abstractions used in explainable AI models. Because they are explicitly defined by humans (e.g., "red wings" or "striped pattern"), they generally require auxiliary datasets that contain ground-truth annotations for these specific concepts during training.
