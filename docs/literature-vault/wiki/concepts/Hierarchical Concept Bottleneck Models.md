# Hierarchical Concept Bottleneck Models

Hierarchical Concept Bottleneck Models introduce a multi-layered structure to the concept bottleneck. Instead of a flat list of concepts, concepts are organized taxonomically or logically (e.g., from broad visual categories to specific fine-grained shapes). This allows the model to make classifications at varying levels of granularity and handles ambiguity more gracefully.
