# Hierarchical Concept Embedding Models (HiCEMs)

HiCEMs are an extension of CEMs that explicitly model hierarchical relationships between concepts. By incorporating positive and negative sub-concept modules, a concept's embedding contains information about its specific sub-concepts, allowing for multi-level test-time interventions.
