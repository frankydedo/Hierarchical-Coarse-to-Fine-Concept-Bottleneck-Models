# Hyperbolic Concept Bottleneck Models (HypCBM)

Hyperbolic Concept Bottleneck Models (HypCBM) embed concepts in a hyperbolic manifold rather than a flat Euclidean space. Because hyperbolic space's volume expands exponentially, it naturally accommodates the tree-like, hierarchical structure of semantic concepts, avoiding the geometric distortions seen in standard CBMs.
