# Concept Splitting

Concept Splitting is a method for automatically discovering finer-grained sub-concepts from a pretrained Concept Embedding Model (CEM). It typically employs Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) or clustering on the concept embeddings to find mutually exclusive or sparse latent features without requiring additional manual annotations.
