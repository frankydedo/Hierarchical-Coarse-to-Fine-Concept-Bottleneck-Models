# Progressive Concept Bottleneck Models (P-CBMs)

Progressive Concept Bottleneck Models (P-CBMs) utilize a multi-stage, sequential sequence of bottlenecks to emulate complex human decision-making processes. For instance, a P-CBM might first predict a [[Segmentation Bottleneck]] to "see" the relevant objects, and then use that to predict a [[Property Bottleneck]] to "conceive" their characteristics before making a final prediction.
