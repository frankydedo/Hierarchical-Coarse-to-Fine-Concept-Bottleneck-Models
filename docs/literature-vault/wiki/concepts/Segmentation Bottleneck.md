# Segmentation Bottleneck

A Segmentation Bottleneck is an intermediate layer in a computer vision model that explicitly outputs spatial segmentation masks for regions of interest. These masks act as visual concepts, forcing the model to ground its subsequent reasoning strictly on the identified anatomical or structural features.
