# Hi-CBM

Hi-CBM is a hierarchical concept bottleneck model designed to mitigate concept leakage. It utilizes a two-level hierarchy of concepts (perceptual and descriptive) and enforces rigorous concept-to-class mappings through an intervention matrix and a selective pooling mechanism.
