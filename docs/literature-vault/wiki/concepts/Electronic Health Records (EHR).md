# Electronic Health Records (EHR)

Electronic Health Records (EHRs) are digital collections of patient health information, including demographics, hospital visits, medical procedures, clinical diagnoses, and prescribed medications (often standardized using coding systems like ICD).

EHR data naturally exhibits a complex, multi-level hierarchical structure:
- **Patient Level:** Contains multiple visits and overall demographics.
- **Visit Level:** Contains multiple medical codes for a specific hospital admission.
- **Code Level:** Represents specific diagnoses, procedures, and medications.

Analyzing EHR data is a primary focus for predictive healthcare modeling, enabling tasks like mortality risk assessment, readmission prediction, and drug recommendation.
